# Data Card

**Dataset:** Age-dependent timing and routes demonstrate developmental plasticity in a long-distance migratory bird

**Authors:** Mo A. Verhoeven, A. H. Jelle Loonstra, Alice D. McBride, Wiebe Kaspersma, Jos C. E. W. Hooijmeijer, Christiaan Both, Nathan R. Senner, and Theunis Piersma

**Repository:** Dryad Digital Repository

**DOI:** 10.5061/dryad.02v6wwq4j

**File used:** `2.Adult_and_juvenile_timing_forR.csv`

**License:** CC0

**Species:** Continental Black-tailed Godwit (*Limosa limosa limosa*)

**Study area:** Southwest Fryslân, the Netherlands.

**Data content:** The file contains migration-timing records for juvenile and adult Black-tailed Godwits at a series of latitudinal boundaries during southward and northward migration.

**Measurement:** Juvenile timing was derived from satellite PTT tracking data. Adult timing was reconstructed from light-level geolocator data using FLightR.

**Intended use in this assignment:** To assess whether the dataset can support a comparison of the timing of crossing 52°N during southward migration between tracked juvenile and adult godwits.

**Analytical subset:** Southward migration records at 52°N from 2016 and 2017. The subset contains 78 migration records from 71 unique individuals.

**Known limitations:** Juvenile and adult timing was obtained using different measurement systems. The final timing sample is not a random subset of all tagged birds. Some individuals contribute observations in more than one year, and data from 2016 and 2017 are pooled.

**Claim boundary:** The dataset can support a descriptive comparison of measured migration timing between the tracked age groups, but it cannot by itself identify the causal mechanism responsible for the difference or justify generalization to all populations and years.
