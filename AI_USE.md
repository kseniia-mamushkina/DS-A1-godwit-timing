**Accepted assistance:**

- Python code for hash calculation, filtering, schema, range, missingness, duplicate checks, anomaly, and leakage checks;

**Rejected or modified assistance:**

Suggested interpretations were also compared with the original article and adjusted where they were too broad or not directly supported by the assignment or source material.

**Independent verification:**  
- Tracking methods and study design were checked against the original Verhoeven et al. article.
- All Python code was executed in Google Colab.
- Outputs were inspected manually for consistency with the source dataset.
- Dataset filtering and record counts were checked against the structure of the CSV and the study description.

**Final responsibility:**  
All interpretations and final wording were reviewed and edited by the student.
